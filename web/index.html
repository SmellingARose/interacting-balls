Techniques

A new network first learns to imitate the algorithm (behaviour cloning with DAgger), then evolves from there.

A new network starts on an easy version of your settings and steps up a level (of 10) each time validation reaches this twice in a row. Level 10 is your settings.

When neither validation nor the training score improves for 6 checks, CMA-ES restarts around the best network, alternating a wide search with a big population and a fine one with a small population.

Rescales each sensor by its running mean and spread. Folded into the network's first layer, so saved networks are unchanged.

CMA-ES variant

Automatic uses sep-CMA-ES up to 2,000 weights and LM-MA-ES above. Both draw mirrored pairs of candidates.
